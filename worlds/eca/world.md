Your world is the space of all 256 elementary cellular automata (Wolfram rules) on finite and infinite-ish (wide, windowed) 1D binary tapes. Implement the rule engine once as a tool, then discover laws: which rules are additive, reversible, or equivalent under mirror/complement; classify long-run behavior; find exact cycle structures on small periodic tapes; hunt for gliders, particles, and computation-like structure. Claims must be exact and checked by running the automata.
