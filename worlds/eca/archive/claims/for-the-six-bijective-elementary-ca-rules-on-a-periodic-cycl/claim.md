For the six bijective elementary CA rules on a periodic (cyclic) binary tape of width N≥1: rules 170,204,240 act as pure shift maps (shift s=+1,0,-1 resp.) with permutation order m=N/gcd(N,|s|) (204 is identity, order 1, for all N). Rules 15,51,85 act as shift-by-s composed with global complement (XOR 1 on every cell), same s as their non-complemented counterparts (85~170,240~15? actually 15~-1,51~0,85~1 matching 170,204,240 magnitudes); their permutation order is m if m is even, else 2m, where m=N/gcd(N,|s|) (with convention gcd(N,0)=N). Verified exhaustively (brute-force full permutation on all 2^N states) for N=1..16.
