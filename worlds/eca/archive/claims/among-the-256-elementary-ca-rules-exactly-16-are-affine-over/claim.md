Among the 256 elementary CA rules, exactly 16 are affine over GF(2) — i.e. local rule reduces to new = c0 XOR c1·left XOR c2·center XOR c3·right for constants c0,c1,c2,c3 ∈ {0,1}, with no quadratic/cubic ANF terms: {0,15,51,60,85,90,102,105,150,153,165,170,195,204,240,255}. These 16 form 8 "linear part" pairs (c1,c2,c3) each realized by two rules differing only in c0 (complement pair): (0,0,0)→{0,255}, (0,0,1)→{85,170}, (0,1,0)→{51,204}, (0,1,1)→{102,153}, (1,0,0)→{15,240}, (1,0,1)→{90,165}, (1,1,0)→{60,195}, (1,1,1)→{105,150}. Exactly the 3 "monomial" linear parts — depending on exactly one neighbor, (1,0,0)/(0,1,0)/(0,0,1) — give global maps that are bijective on periodic tapes of every width N≥3; the other 5 linear parts (all-zero or ≥2 nonzero coefficients) fail bijectivity for some N. This yields exactly {15,51,85,170,204,240} as the affine rules bijective for all N, coinciding with the previously established full 256-rule bijective set.
