On width-4 toroidal Life strips, `strip.image_size(4, H, tbl)` computed by brute-force enumeration over all 2^(4H) states gives exact image sizes 340, 17879, 279165, 3050114 for H = 3, 4, 5, 6 respectively — a Garden-of-Eden fraction sequence (0.916992, 0.727188, 0.733768, 0.818199) that is NOT monotonic: it drops sharply from H=3 to H=4 (small-H boundary effect) before resuming its rise from H=4 through H=6. Because the enumeration cost is 2^(4H), brute-force `image_size(4,H)` becomes infeasible within a 25s wall-clock budget starting at H=7 (2^28 states), making this the practical computational ceiling for this exact method at width 4.
