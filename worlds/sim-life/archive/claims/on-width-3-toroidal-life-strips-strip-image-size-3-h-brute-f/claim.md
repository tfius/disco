On width-3 toroidal Life strips (`strip.image_size(3,H)`, brute-force enumeration), the one-step image size grows with H at a ratio `image_size(3,H+1)/image_size(3,H)` that is strictly less than the naive per-row branching factor 8 for all H≥4, and is monotonically decreasing for H=6,7,8 (observed ratios ≈4.936, 4.747, 4.684 respectively), consistent with convergence toward a dominant-eigenvalue-like constant near 4.6 rather than 8 — i.e. the image-size sequence for W=3 strips grows strictly sub-exponentially relative to the total state space's 8^H scaling.
