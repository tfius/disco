On toroidal Life grids the two smallest square toruses are degenerate and their one-step image is known exactly: on 2×2, wraparound makes every neighborhood sum even so births are impossible, and the image of the step map is exactly the 5 states {empty} ∪ {4 domino pairs}, every one a fixed point (11/16 states are Gardens of Eden). On 3×3, each cell's 8 neighbors are precisely the 8 other cells, so dynamics depend only on population: the image is exactly {empty, full} ∪ {all 126 population-4 states} (128/512), fixed points are exactly {empty} ∪ pop-4 (127), every population-3 state maps to the full grid, and the full grid maps to empty. On the first generic sizes (all 8 neighbor offsets distinct) Garden-of-Eden states dominate and the reachable fraction resumes decreasing with area: 4×4 has exactly 17879 reachable states of 65536 (53 fixed points), 4×5 has exactly 279165 of 1048576 (313 fixed points), so reachable fraction drops 0.2728 → 0.2662 and the GoE fraction is ≈73% at both sizes.
