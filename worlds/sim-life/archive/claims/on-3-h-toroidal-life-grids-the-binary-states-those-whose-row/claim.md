On 3×H toroidal Life grids, the "binary" states — those whose rows are each all-alive (pop 3) or all-dead (pop 0) — are closed under the Life step, and on them the dynamics is exactly elementary cellular automaton Rule 22 on a ring of H cells (a row is full at t+1 iff exactly one of {row above, row itself, row below} is full at t). Consequently the binary cycle spectrum of the 3×H torus equals the Rule 22 ring-of-H cycle spectrum; exhaustively for H=4..10 this yields, contrary to the naive expectation that width-3 supports only periods 1–2, oscillators of periods 4, 6, and 7 — e.g. at H=7 the binary state with full rows {0,2,6} is a period-7 "phase wave" whose single Life step equals a rigid shift by +3 rows. Moreover, the full reduced row-population automaton on {0,1,2,3}^H (valid by the archived width-3 factorization) has, for H=4..10, every cycle lifting to a true full-grid oscillator of exactly the same period when a rep state with row pops (p_r) is lifted by filling the first p_r columns of each row — no period multiplication ever occurs.
