On 32×32 toroidal Life grids, random density-0.5 soups (seeds 900000..900019, `life.soup(seed,32,32,0.5)`, evolved via `cyc.find_cycle` to their attractor cycle) have ash dominated by frozen (still-life) cells: across these 20 seeds, mean frozen-cell density (frozen cells / 1024) is 0.0300 (range [0.0127, 0.0791]), and mean fraction of average live population that is frozen (intersection of all cycle-frame live-sets, divided by mean live count per frame) is 0.841, with every seed's frac_frozen ≥ 0.5. Cycle periods observed for these seeds are only {1, 2, 128} — i.e. mostly fixed points or period-2 blinker-type oscillators, with one longer-period (128) outlier.
