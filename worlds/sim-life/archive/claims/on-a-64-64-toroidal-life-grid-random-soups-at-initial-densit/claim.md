On a 64×64 toroidal Life grid, random soups at initial density 0.5 (cells set where `random.Random(seed).random() < 0.5`, column-major x-then-y order) always self-organize into low-density periodic ash: for every seed 0–9, the trajectory enters a cycle of period exactly 2 (still lifes plus blinker-type oscillators; period-2 absorbs period-1 states) within 3,700 generations, and the settled density lands in [0.015, 0.045] with a 10-seed mean of 0.0277 ± 0.005 — a ~95% population collapse from the initial 0.5. No high-period or wandering-spaceship attractor appeared in any of the 10 trajectories, and anomalously slow seeds (1 and 3, still active at gen 2000) merely take longer, cycling by gen ~3,700 with densities that regress to the same band.
