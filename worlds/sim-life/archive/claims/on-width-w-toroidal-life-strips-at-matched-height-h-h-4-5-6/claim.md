On width-W toroidal Life strips at matched height H (H=4,5,6), the exact Garden-of-Eden fraction (1 − image/2^(WH), via `strip.image_size(W,H)`) is *not* monotonically increasing in W: width-3 strips have strictly higher GoE fraction than width-4 strips at every one of H=4,5,6 — W=3: {H4:0.916992, H5:0.950500, H6:0.969452} vs W=4: {H4:0.727188, H5:0.733768, H6:0.818199}. This contradicts the naive expectation that wider strips (more neighborhood combinatorics) always have a higher GoE fraction — within this H range, narrower W=3 dominates.
