`strip.image_size(W, H, tbl)` runs in time proportional to 2^(W·H) (total state count of the W×H torus), essentially independent of how the product splits between W and H: at matched product W·H, runtimes agree within a small constant factor (e.g. W·H=20: 0.118s vs 0.111s; W·H=24: 2.354s vs 1.990s), at a roughly constant throughput of ~7–9×10^6 states/sec on the reference machine. Consequently width-5 strips are only tractable up to about H=5 (2^25 states, ~4s); H=6 (2^30 states) would take on the order of 2 minutes, exceeding the 30s experiment budget, which is what caused the original TIMEOUT for the H=3..9 sweep.
