On W×H toroidal Life grids the exact number of one-step-reachable states ("image size", complement of Gardens of Eden) is computable by row-by-row scan (`strip.image_size`, valid only for H≥3; H=2 raises IndexError at `head[1]`) and cross-validates against brute-force stepping of all 2^(WH) states. Exact values: image(2,H)=10, 89, 293, 862, 3251, 10961, 36058 for H=3..9; image(3,H)=128, 340, 1622, 8008, 38012, 178072 for H=3..8; image(4,4)=17879, image(4,5)=279165, image(4,6)=3050114; image(5,5)=8520996. Image size is symmetric under transpose: image(W,H)=image(H,W) (verified at (4,5)/(5,4), (4,6)/(6,4), (3,4)/(4,3)). The per-cell ratio r(W,H)=image^(1/(WH)) is NON-monotone in both arguments: width 3 is anomalously constrained, converging near 1.655 (matching the strip-DFA growth rate 4.544950^(1/3)) — below width 2's limit ≈1.803 (3.250988^(1/2)) — while widths 4-5 sit near 1.84-1.89; and at fixed W=4 there is a dip, r(4,6)=1.8629 < r(4,5)=1.8719. All ratios stay below 2, so the Garden-of-Eden fraction 1−image/2^(WH) tends to 1 as area grows in every measured family.
