On N×N toroidal Life grids, the R-pentomino `{(0,1),(0,2),(1,0),(1,1),(2,1)}` placed at offset (N//2−1, N//2−1) always settles into a cycle, and its period is governed entirely by glider survival: the ash is a period-≤2 background plus k ≥ 0 surviving gliders, each of which is a 5-cell 8-connected cluster that reappears translated by (±1,±1) after exactly 4 generations and therefore has torus period exactly 4N. Consequently **period > 2 ⟺ k ≥ 1, and whenever k ≥ 1 the period is exactly 4N** (never 8N, never 2N, never an lcm with an odd factor — multi-glider cases give 4N too, since all gliders share the same 4N period and the background period divides 2). Deleting a Chebyshev-radius-3 halo around all glider cells from the cycle state leaves a background identical at t and t+2. Verified over N = 100..175 (glider sizes include 108, 110, 112, 116, 124, 125, 128–132, 145, 151, 166–175; period-2 sizes include 100–107, 113–115, 141–144, 150, 160), with the biconditional holding without exception.
