On the 4×4 toroidal Life grid (65536 states), the functional graph is exactly: 47657 states (72.72%) are Gardens of Eden; the attractors are 53 fixed points, 180 period-2 cycles, 16 period-4 cycles, and 96 period-8 cycles; the maximum transient depth is 9. The period-4 and period-8 cycles close through symmetry in qualitatively different ways. Every period-4 cycle is a translating wave: s(t+1) is a pure translation of s(t) by a vector whose minimal mod-4 representative has Chebyshev norm 2 (e.g. (2,3) ≡ (2,−1)), i.e. these waves displace 2 cells per generation, exceeding the open-plane speed limit c=1 — possible only because the finite-pattern speed-limit argument fails under toroidal wrap. No period-8 cycle is translation-closed at half-period; instead every one of the 96 satisfies s(t+4) = g·s(t) where g is rot180 composed with a translation — they close only through a point-symmetry twist. Under the 128-element symmetry group (16 translations × D4) the cycles fall into 6 + 8 + 1 + 2 orbits by period, with the period-8 orbit sizes 64 + 32 (stabilizers of order 2 and 4).
