On 3×H toroidal Life grids (state encoding bit = r·3+c, verified against `life.step` on grids-as-row-bitmask-lists), the exact cycle spectrum of the full 2^(3H)-state dynamics for H = 2..7 is: H=2 → 3 fixed points only; H=3 → 127 fixed points only; H=4 → 39 fixed points plus 2 period-2 cycles; H=5 → 121 fixed points only; H=6 → 2595 fixed points only; H=7 → 1177 fixed points, 63 period-4 cycles, and 2 period-7 cycles. Consequently oscillator existence is non-monotone in H: periods >1 exist at H=4 and H=7 but at no other H ≤ 7. Structurally at H=7: all 63 period-4 cycles are true oscillators (zero net translation over a period) and fall into exactly 3 equivalence classes of size 21 under the free action of the 3·7 = 21 torus translations; the 2 period-7 cycles are "binary" states (every row 000 or 111) that are pure traveling waves — one satisfies g(t+1) = g(t) shifted down 3 rows, the other shifted down 4 rows — i.e. lifts of rule-22 waves on the 7-ring, consistent with the archived width-3 row-population factoring.
