On square N×N toroidal Life grids, the lightweight spaceship (LWSS, the 9-cell pattern `{(0,1),(0,4),(1,0),(2,0),(2,4),(3,0),(3,1),(3,2),(3,3)}`) survives as a genuine constant-velocity spaceship — reproducing its exact shape translated by `(0, N-2)` every period (4 generations), linearly for 10 consecutive periods with no self-collision — for exactly N ∈ {11, 13, 15, 17, 19} ∪ {N : N ≥ 21}, i.e. all odd N ≥ 11 plus all N ≥ 21 regardless of parity; it fails (collides with its own wrapped image, offset degenerating to (0,0)) for all N ≤ 10 and for the even N ∈ {12, 14, 16, 18, 20}. The failure band is a finite, non-monotonic artifact of wraparound self-interaction at small-to-medium even tori, not a parity law — it closes permanently once N ≥ 21.
