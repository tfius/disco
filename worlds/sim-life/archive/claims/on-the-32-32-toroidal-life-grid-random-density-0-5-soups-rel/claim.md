On the 32×32 toroidal Life grid, random density-0.5 soups relax to short cycles whose period spectrum is dominated by periods 1 and 2 in roughly a 1:3.5 ratio, with a ~1% tail of period-128 glider attractors and no other periods at all. Precisely: using exact full-state cycle detection (hash every visited state, cap 6000 generations), the 100 soups `life.soup(seed, 32, 32, 0.5)` for seed in 910000..910099 all converge with period spectrum exactly {1: 21, 2: 78, 128: 1} (transient mean 516.0, max 2224), and the 100 soups for seed in 920000..920099 all converge with spectrum exactly {1: 22, 2: 77, 128: 1} (transient mean 444.5, max 1471). No soup in either batch has a period in 3..127 or above 128; each batch's single long-period attractor has period exactly 128 = 4·32, the signature of a glider circulating the torus. (The earlier contradictory result came from a cycle-detection cap of 4096 generations, which is insufficient; transient+period here can exceed that, and exact detection to 6000 resolves it.)
