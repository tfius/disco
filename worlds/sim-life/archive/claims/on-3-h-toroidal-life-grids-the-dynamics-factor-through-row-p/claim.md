On 3×H toroidal Life grids the dynamics factor through row populations: since width 3 makes every cell's neighborhood cover all three columns, a cell at row y sees S−(own state) live neighbors where S = pop(row y−1)+pop(row y)+pop(row y+1), so the successor of row y is all-ones (111) if S=3, an exact copy of row y if S=4, and empty otherwise. Consequences, all exact: (a) on 3×3 the neighborhood is the whole grid, so the still lifes are precisely the empty grid plus every 4-cell pattern — C(9,4)+1 = 127; (b) the still-life count N(3,H) equals trace(M^H) for the 16-state transfer matrix M on (prev-row-pop, row-pop) pairs with entry C(3,b) for each valid continuation b, matching brute-force enumeration (N=127, 39, 121, 2595 at H=3,4,5,6 and 8112423 at H=16); (c) growth splits by H mod 3: for 3|H the ratio N(H)/N(H−3) increases monotonically toward 27 (26.978 at H=48, never exceeding 27), giving entropy log2N/(3H) → log2(3)/3 ≈ 0.5283 from above, while for 3∤H the ratio locks at 19.7625±0.001 (entropy → 0.4783), so the dominant transfer eigenvalue-triple has cube-root-of-unity structure that only heights divisible by 3 can exploit.
