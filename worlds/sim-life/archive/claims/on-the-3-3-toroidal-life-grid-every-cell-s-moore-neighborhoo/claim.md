On the 3×3 toroidal Life grid every cell's Moore neighborhood covers the whole grid, so the dynamics depend only on total population T: the image of `step` is exactly the 128 states {empty} ∪ {all-alive} ∪ {all 126 population-4 states}, leaving exactly 384 Gardens of Eden (including 9 of population 1); all 126 pop-4 states are fixed points, all 84 pop-3 states map to all-alive, and all-alive maps to empty. On the 3×4 and 3×5 toruses (state encoding bit = r·3+c, dynamics `w3census.step_int`) the preimage set of the all-alive state is exactly the set of states in which every row has population exactly 1, hence has size 3^H (81 and 243) — so all-alive is reachable on 3×H but, on the 4×4 torus (`torus4.build_f(4)`), all-alive is not reachable and the minimum-population Gardens of Eden have population 2, being exactly the 32 diagonally-adjacent live pairs (displacement classes (1,1) and (1,3) mod 4, 16 translations each); every other population-2 state on 4×4 is reachable.
