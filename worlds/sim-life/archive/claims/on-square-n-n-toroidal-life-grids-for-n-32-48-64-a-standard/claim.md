On square N×N toroidal Life grids for N ∈ {32,48,64}, a standard glider `{(0,1),(1,2),(2,0),(2,1),(2,2)}` anchored at (0,0) collides with a mirrored glider `{(0,1),(1,0),(2,0),(2,1),(2,2)}` anchored at (0,d) (same row, column offset d), and for every offset d in 4..13 the post-collision outcome after 400 steps depends only on d mod 2, independent of N: even d always settles to population 8 at period 1 (a stable still-life/block-like remnant), odd d always settles to population 18 at period 2 (a two-phase oscillating remnant) — the two gliders' relative column parity, not their absolute separation or the torus size, determines the collision product.
