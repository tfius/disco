On square NxN toroidal Life grids for N=6..12, random density-0.5 soups (150 soups per N, seeds 810000..810149, `cyc.spectrum`, cap=8000) never relax to an odd oscillation period greater than 1 — every observed period across 1050 trials was 1, 2, or a multiple of 4 (with one single exception: N=8 produced one period-6 case), consistent with the earlier 9×9/13×13 spectra (300 soups each, seeds 800000..800299) which likewise showed zero odd periods >1. The dominant even non-trivial periods (28, 32, 36, 40, 44, 48, 132) are consistently multiples of 4·N or otherwise even, matching glider/spaceship wrap-around drift signatures rather than intrinsic odd oscillators.
