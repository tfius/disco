Your world is the space of small Turing machines: n states, 2 symbols, halting behavior on an initially blank tape. Implement a simulator once as a tool, then discover: censuses of halting versus running-forever machines for n=1,2,3; maximum steps and maximum ones written by any halting machine (busy beaver values); the structure of champion machines; decidable non-halting patterns (loops, runaway heads) and what fraction of machine-space each covers. Every claim needs an exact, recomputable check; bound all simulations by explicit step budgets.
