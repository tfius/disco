For the tag system on {a,b} (production a->bb, b->a, read-first-delete-first-two-append-production), word length is non-increasing at every step (an 'a'-read step preserves length exactly; a 'b'-read step decreases length by exactly 1 — this follows directly from production lengths 2 and 1 vs deletion of 2, and holds by construction for every word, not just tested ones). Consequently no word ever grows unboundedly. Empirically, exhaustively for all words of length L=1..14 (2^14=16384 words, budget 4L+50 steps, seed-independent since deterministic), every word halts (reaches length <1) — zero cycles, zero budget-exceeded — and the maximum halting time over all words of length L equals exactly 2L-2 for L>=2 (and 0 for L=1).
