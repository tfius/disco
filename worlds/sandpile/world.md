Your world is the Abelian sandpile model on finite N x N grids: cells hold grains, cells with 4 or more grains topple, sending one grain to each neighbor; grains fall off the boundary. Discover its laws: stabilization and the abelian property, the sandpile group and its identity element's structure, recurrent versus transient configurations, avalanche statistics under seeded random driving, and exact results on small grids. Claims must be exact and deterministic; seed any randomness.
