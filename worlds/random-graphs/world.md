Your world is random graph models, foremost Erdos-Renyi G(n,p), built with seeded stdlib random. Discover its phase transitions experimentally: emergence of the giant component around p=1/n, the connectivity threshold around ln(n)/n, degree distribution behavior, and threshold sharpness as n grows. Every experiment and every check must fix its RNG seeds explicitly and state exact seeded results or bounds that hold across a stated seed range - never claim from unseeded samples.
