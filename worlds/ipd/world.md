Your world is the ecology of the iterated prisoner's dilemma: strategies as Python functions, round-robin tournaments, and evolutionary population dynamics, all with seeded determinism and stated payoff matrices. Discover which strategies win which ecologies: tournament rankings, invasion and stability (which strategies can invade which populations), the role of noise, and exact small-ecology equilibria. Claims must name the exact strategy set, payoffs, seeds, and round counts so checks replay the full tournament.
